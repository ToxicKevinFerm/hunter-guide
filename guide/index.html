        Step 5

        What to look for on gear

        Get 8% hit first. After that, compare items with this order: longer bar, better stat.

        Weapons

        • Weave: a slow two-hander with a slow bow, e.g. Arcanite Reaper with Bloodseeker.
        • Turret: Bloodseeker, a free quest reward and practically the same as Eaglehorn Long Bow.
        • Melee: dual wield, Crusader on the main hand.

        Survival's Surefooted gives 3% hit, so Survival needs only 3–5% from gear.

        Words you'll see

        Turret
        Stand at range and shoot. Nothing else.
        Weave
        Walk in for one melee swing between Auto Shots, then walk back out.
        Lone Wolf
        A talent: dismiss your pet, deal 20% more damage.
        Dead zone
        Too close to shoot, too far to hit. Step past about 13 yd.
        Hit cap
        8% hit stops you missing a raid boss. More is wasted.
        DPS
        Damage per second, averaged over thousands of simulated fights.
